Scope: `/offer/` catalog page `https://tacticum.ru/offer/`, public filters, quick entries/presets, taxonomy ownership, synthetic offer examples, budget display, SEO/indexability and future Bitrix content model.

## Purpose

Этот документ фиксирует результаты придирчивого challenge страницы `Примеры расчетов` and answers the key architecture question: whether filters and presets should move from PHP-derived heuristics into Bitrix iblocks.

Главный вывод: переносить нужно не текущие фильтры one-to-one, а управляемую taxonomy/preset layer. Counts, availability and result sets must remain derived from active offer items.

## Challenge Verdict

Current implementation is technically coherent for a generated catalog, but product governance is weak.

The page is not failing because filters are computed in PHP. It is failing because public taxonomy is implicitly produced by offer payload text and seed dictionaries. This makes navigation, labels and slugs dependent on implementation data instead of an approved public vocabulary.

Transferring the whole current filter state to an iblock would be the wrong fix. It would freeze an unreviewed taxonomy, create admin overhead and risk empty/false categories. The right fix is to separate three layers:

| Layer | Target Owner | Should Be In Bitrix? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer examples | Offer iblock / generator / calculator source | Already in offer iblock | Remains source of factual examples and raw payloads. |
| Public taxonomy terms | Content + PM + SEO + Architect | Yes, as taxonomy terms or equivalent governed model | Owns labels, slugs, aliases, sort order, visibility and featured flags. |
| Counts and filtered results | Runtime service | No | Must remain derived from active offer items to avoid empty navigation. |

## Target Public Role For `/offer/`

Recommended role:

> `/offer/` is an estimate-orientation catalog: it helps a buyer compare similar AI/IT initiatives by industry, scenario, budget range and launch format, then move to a calculator or exact assessment.

It should not read as:

- final commercial offer repository;
- real customer proof unless evidence is approved;
- arbitrary synthetic taxonomy dump;
- SEO landing-page generator without content owner approval.

## Do Not Start Without Gates

- Do not move raw current filter values into Bitrix as approved public vocabulary without Content/PM/SEO review.
- Do not store counts in Bitrix taxonomy rows; counts must be derived from active offers.
- Do not make filtered URLs indexable just because taxonomy is governed.
- Do not change offer detail canonical/sitemap behavior in a taxonomy-only task.
- Do not present synthetic examples as real customer proof without Legal/Sales approval.
- Do not change calculator, offer form payload, CRM/upstream fields or analytics events as part of taxonomy migration.
- Do not hardcode new iblock IDs; use config registry helpers.
